About Us

A tiny parlor with a big obsession

Dream Scoops began in a home kitchen with one secondhand churner and a stubborn belief that ice cream should taste like the fruit it came from.

Today we still make every batch by hand, in quantities small enough to check each tub before it leaves the counter. No powders, no shortcuts, nothing that sits frozen for a month.

  • Churned in batches of 12 litres or fewer
  • Fruit sourced from Guimaras and Benguet growers
  • Compostable cups, spoons and cones
